Support

We want Xclr to work well for your store. Here is what support covers and how to reach us.

What support includes

  • Installing and setting up the theme.
  • Questions about the settings and sections described in this documentation.
  • Fixing bugs in unmodified theme code.
  • Help understanding theme behavior that is not covered in this documentation.

What support does not include

  • Custom code changes, custom sections, or redesigns.
  • Third-party apps, their setup, or conflicts caused by apps.
  • Store setup, product uploads, content writing, or marketing.
  • Changes to the theme that were made after delivery.

Before you contact support

  1. Check the FAQ and the theme settings reference.
  2. Reproduce the problem with your most recent code changes or newly installed apps disabled, to see whether an app causes it.
  3. Try a second browser and a mobile width to see whether the problem is specific to one environment.

What to include in your request

  • Your store URL and the theme version (shown in Theme settings and in this documentation's footer).
  • The page and section where the problem appears, with a link if possible.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem, and what you expected to happen.
  • Browser, device, and screen width.
  • A screenshot or short screen recording.

Support policy. Support responds within 2 business days, Monday to Friday. Bug fixes are provided in future theme versions. Custom work can be discussed separately.
