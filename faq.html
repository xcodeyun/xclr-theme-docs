Frequently asked questions

How do I change my logo, fonts, or colors?

Open the theme editor and click the settings icon. Logo and favicon are under Branding, the font is under Typography, and colors are under Colors.

What image sizes should I use?

ImageRecommended size
Hero banner2400 × 1600 px or larger, landscape
Image with text1600 × 2000 px or larger, portrait
Product images1200 × 1600 px or larger, portrait 3:4
Collection images1200 × 1200 px or larger, square
LogoHorizontal, transparent PNG or SVG, at least 2× the displayed width
Favicon48 × 48 px or larger, square

The theme generates responsive image sizes automatically, so larger source images are safe to upload.

Why is my featured collection empty?

Check two things: a collection is selected in the section settings, and that collection contains products. The section stays empty when no collection is chosen.

Why does my banner image look cropped?

The hero image fills the banner and is cropped around the center. Use a wider image and keep the subject away from the edges, or raise the overlay opacity so text stays readable.

Can I add apps?

This version of the theme does not include app blocks. Apps that output Liquid can be inserted with the Custom liquid section, following the app's own installation instructions.

Does the theme support multiple languages?

Storefront text from the theme is translatable through locale files. Version 0.1.2 includes English only; additional languages can be added by adding locale files in the theme code.

How do I update the theme?

Upload the new .zip file as a new theme, preview it, and publish it when you are ready. Keep a copy of any code customizations so you can re-apply them.

Can I use the theme on more than one store?

Each theme license covers one store. Contact support if you need to use Xclr on additional stores.

How do I report a bug?

Email xcodeyun@gmail.com with your store URL, a description of the problem, and a screenshot. See Support for what to include.
